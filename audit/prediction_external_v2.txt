External benchmark v2 (semi-synthetic) - design and predictions recorded BEFORE any model is fitted (2026-11-23)
=====================================================================================================================
Why a second design (review of 2026-11-23, point 7): in v1 the reference difference was computed on the same 39 cells
from which the small test sets were drawn (finite-population factor 0.72), so CR2 over-covered and coverage was not a
test of calibration; the reference was itself one clustered sample. Here labels are generated by a known model, the
small test sets are independent draws from a population, and the reference is computed on a disjoint sample of the
SAME population, so large that its Monte Carlo error is negligible. v1 is NOT replaced: it was recorded before any fit
(commit 1e88a96) and stays in the paper as recorded, with its over-coverage stated; v2 is added as the corrected design.
How it differs from the simulations of the paper (Figures 4 and 5): those use binormal scores on an abstract cluster
design. Here we retain the observed covariates, unequal cell sizes, and the predictions of four fixed fitted models.
Labels are simulated, including any residual within-cell dependence, and cell draws are independent by construction.
What the real data keep is the dependence between the models' scores; their errors also depend on the simulated
labels.
What it tests: coverage and false decisions of the inference, not which model is better. The generating model is an
XGBoost fit, so it favours XGBoost by construction; no ranking of the four models is read from this benchmark.

Covariates and clusters: California Housing (scikit-learn), the 8 features and the 0.25 x 0.25 deg cells of v1, the
  same split by cell (seed 2026: 259 training, 77 validation, 182 test cells). No real outcome is used after step 1.
Step 1 - the generator (training cells only):
  f(x)  = logit of an XGBoost classifier (depth 4, learning rate 0.05, 300 rounds, seed 0) fitted to the real binary
          target of v1 on the training rows only, then frozen. It is the 'true' signal; LR is misspecified for it.
  Cell effects: for cell c, u_c ~ N(0, s_u^2) (intercept) and v_c ~ N(0, s_v^2) (slope on f), independent across
          cells. The slope makes the models' errors differ between cells (scorer-specific cell effects), the mechanism
          under which DeLong under-covered in the panel simulation; s_v = s_u / 2 is a judgement, not an estimate.
  Labels: y = 1[ f(x)(1 + v_c) + u_c + logistic noise > 0 ].
Step 1b - the scale s_u, three scenarios fixed here (s_v = s_u / 2 in all of them):
  S0  s_u = s_v = 0: no residual dependence of the labels given the covariates, and no other shared random term
      (u_c and v_c are both identically zero). This is NOT an iid design (cases in a cell share covariates, and the
      models' scores remain dependent within cells) and NOT a null of the AUC difference.
  S2  s_u = s*: the value that reproduces the dependence observed in v1, where on the 182 whole test cells the CR2
      standard error was 4.4-6.6 times the DeLong one. Calibration pilot, with seeds disjoint from steps 2-4: for each
      s_u on the grid 0.25, 0.50, ..., 2.00, the four models (seed 0) are fitted on one synthetic label draw of the
      training and validation cells; then, over 200 synthetic label draws on the 182 test-cell covariate blocks (the
      cell-size distribution of v1; covariates only, no real outcome), the median ratio SE_CR2 / SE_DeLong of the
      declared contrasts C1-C3 is computed. s* is the grid value whose ratio, averaged over C1-C3, is closest to 5.5,
      the midpoint of v1. If no grid value reaches 4.4, the largest is used and this is reported. The ratio is
      calibrated directly; no intraclass correlation of labels is used to translate it, since with unequal cell
      sizes a mean cell size does not carry the ratio over.
  S1  s_u = s* / 2: intermediate dependence.
  s_u is then frozen. The ratio that results in the small tests of step 4 is reported, without requiring it to
  match v1.
Step 2 - models (seeds 0-4), per scenario, fitted on synthetic labels of the training and validation cells, one label
  draw (seed 2027): LR, RF, XGB and MLP with the settings of v1. Frozen afterwards.
Step 3 - one population for reference and tests: the 39 test cells with 10-60 rows (as in v1). A synthetic cell is
  one of these 39 covariate blocks drawn WITH replacement. A block can therefore appear more than once, in a small
  test or in the reference; each appearance receives its own cluster ID and its own independently generated u_c, v_c
  and labels, so cells are i.i.d. draws from a single population and there is no finite-population correction.
  Results refer to this population of 39 blocks, not to California or to all test cells.
  Reference: 20,000 synthetic cells (seed 3001) simulated independently from the SAME population as the small tests.
  True difference of a contrast: the 5-seed mean AUC difference on the pooled reference sample; its Monte Carlo SE is
  estimated by 20 independent batches of 1,000 cells and reported, and must be below 0.001 (otherwise the reference
  sample is enlarged, not the design changed). The true differences
  cannot be known before the models of step 2 exist; they are computed and recorded in audit/truth_external_v2.json,
  in a SECOND commit and an independent timestamped deposit, before any small test of step 4 is scored. The
  difference of C0 is zero by construction.
Step 4 - small tests: per scenario, 2,000 draws (seed 3002) of 19 synthetic cells from the same population (about
  570 rows). In each draw: CR2 (Bell-McCaffrey df, clusters = cells) and DeLong intervals on 5-seed means; resolved
  or not; sign; coverage of the true difference.
Step 5 - a priori MDE: before step 4, the MDE of each contrast is predicted from Hanley-McNeil at the median small-
  test size and prevalence, with the AUCs and AUC correlation of the validation cells, inflated by the design effect
  1 + (m - 1) rho with m the median rows per cell and rho estimated on the validation cells.
Contrasts (fixed): C1 RF - LR, C2 XGB - RF, C3 MLP - RF. Margin: 0.01 AUC. Primary procedure: CR2. 95 %.
Null check: C0 RF(draw A) - RF(draw B), the second fit on an independent label draw of the training and validation
  cells (true difference zero by construction).

Predictions (per scenario unless stated):
P1  The true difference of C1 exceeds the margin in every scenario (LR is misspecified for f).
P2  CR2 coverage of the true difference is 0.93-0.97 for every contrast in every scenario (2,000 draws: MC SE about
    0.005).
P3  In S2, DeLong covers the true difference less often than CR2 for every contrast. (DeLong coverage in S0 and S1
    is reported as a diagnostic; no threshold is set for it, since no independent pilot justifies one.)
P4  For C0, CR2 rejects in 3-7 % of draws in every scenario; DeLong rejects in more than 7 % in S2.
P5  For contrasts whose true difference is within the margin, CR2 resolves in at most 10 % of draws; resolutions with
    the wrong sign are at most 2.5 % of draws for CR2 in every contrast.
P6  The a priori MDE of step 5 is within a factor 0.7-1.4 of the empirical MDE (2.80 x SD of the small-test
    differences) for every contrast in every scenario.
