External benchmark v2 (semi-synthetic) - design and predictions recorded BEFORE any model is fitted (2026-11-23)
=====================================================================================================================
Why a second design (review of 2026-11-23, point 7): in v1 the reference difference was computed on the same 39 cells
from which the small test sets were drawn (finite-population factor 0.72), so CR2 over-covered and coverage was not a
test of calibration; the reference was itself one clustered sample. Here labels are generated by a known model, the
small test sets are independent draws from a population, and the reference is computed on a disjoint sample so large
that its Monte Carlo error is negligible. v1 is NOT replaced: it was recorded before any fit (commit 1e88a96) and stays
in the paper as recorded, with its over-coverage stated; v2 is added as the corrected design.
How it differs from the simulations of the paper (Figures 4 and 5): those use binormal scores on an abstract cluster
design; here the covariates, the spatial cells with their real unequal sizes, and the four fitted models are real, so
the dependence between test cases and between the models' errors comes from real spatial structure, and only the
labels are generated.

Covariates and clusters: California Housing (scikit-learn), the 8 features and the 0.25 x 0.25 deg cells of v1, the
  same split by cell (seed 2026: 259 training, 77 validation, 182 test cells). No real outcome is used after step 1.
Step 1 - the generator (training cells only):
  f(x)  = logit of an XGBoost classifier (depth 4, learning rate 0.05, 300 rounds, seed 0) fitted to the real binary
          target of v1 on the training rows only, then frozen. It is the 'true' signal; LR is misspecified for it.
  Cell effects: for cell c, u_c ~ N(0, s_u^2) (intercept) and v_c ~ N(0, s_v^2) (slope on f), independent across cells.
  s_u and s_v are set from the training cells only: s_u^2 = the variance between training cells of the mean residual
  logit of f (method of moments, floored at 0.25), s_v = s_u / 2.
  Labels: y = 1[ f(x)(1 + v_c) + u_c + logistic noise > 0 ].
Step 2 - models (seeds 0-4) fitted on synthetic labels of the training and validation cells, one draw (seed 2027):
  LR, RF, XGB and MLP with the settings of v1. Frozen afterwards.
Step 3 - population and reference: a synthetic cell is a test-cell covariate block drawn WITH replacement from the 182
  test cells, with fresh u_c, v_c and labels (so cells are i.i.d. draws from one population and there is no finite-
  population correction). Reference sample: 20,000 synthetic cells (seed 3001), disjoint from every small test by
  construction. True difference of a contrast: the 5-seed mean AUC difference on the pooled reference sample; its
  Monte Carlo SE is reported and must be below 0.001 (otherwise the reference sample is enlarged, not the design
  changed). The true differences cannot be known before the models of step 2 exist; they are therefore computed and
  recorded in audit/truth_external_v2.json, in a SECOND commit, before any small test of step 4 is scored. The
  difference of C0 is zero by construction.
Step 4 - small tests: 2,000 draws (seed 3002) of 19 synthetic cells from the test blocks with 10-60 rows (as in v1;
  about 570 rows). In each draw: CR2 (Bell-McCaffrey df, clusters = cells) and DeLong intervals on 5-seed means;
  resolved or not; sign; coverage of the reference difference.
Step 5 - a priori MDE: before step 4, the MDE of each contrast is predicted from Hanley-McNeil at the median small-
  test size and prevalence, with the AUCs and AUC correlation of the validation cells, inflated by the design effect
  1 + (m - 1) rho with m the median rows per cell and rho estimated on the validation cells.
Contrasts (fixed): C1 RF - LR, C2 XGB - RF, C3 MLP - RF. Margin: 0.01 AUC. Primary procedure: CR2. 95 %.
Null check: the same small tests with one model's predictions replaced by a second fit of the same model on an
  independent label draw (true difference zero by construction): C0 RF(draw A) - RF(draw B).

Predictions:
P1  The reference difference of C1 exceeds the margin (LR is misspecified for f).
P2  CR2 coverage of the reference difference is 0.93-0.97 for every contrast (2,000 draws: MC SE about 0.005).
P3  DeLong coverage is below 0.90 for every contrast.
P4  For C0, CR2 rejects in 3-7 % of draws; DeLong rejects in more than 7 %.
P5  For contrasts whose reference is within the margin, CR2 resolves in at most 10 % of draws; resolutions with the
    wrong sign are at most 2.5 % of draws for CR2 in every contrast.
P6  The a priori MDE of step 5 is within a factor 0.7-1.4 of the empirical MDE (2.80 x SD of the small-test
    differences) for every contrast.
