External benchmark - design and predictions recorded BEFORE any model is fitted (2026-11-18)
==========================================================================================
Purpose (review of 2026-11-18, addition 1): a benchmark with real signal and a test set large enough to give a
reference estimate, in order to ask which decisions the protocol would have taken with small clustered test sets.

Data: California Housing (scikit-learn fetch_california_housing; 20,640 block groups, 8 features).
Target: median house value above the median of the training rows (binary).
Clusters: spatial cells of 0.25 deg latitude x 0.25 deg longitude (518 cells; checked before this file: only
  the number of cells and rows per cell, no outcome).
Split by cell (seed 2026): 50 % of cells train, 15 % validation, 35 % test. No cell is in two partitions.
Models (seeds 0-4; LR is deterministic and enters as one seed repeated):
  LR   logistic regression on standardized features (C = 1)
  RF   random forest, 300 trees, min_samples_leaf 5
  XGB  XGBoost hist, depth 6, learning rate 0.05, up to 2000 rounds, early stopping on the validation AUC
  MLP  sklearn MLPClassifier (64, 32), early stopping, standardized features
Contrasts (fixed): C1 RF - LR, C2 XGB - RF, C3 MLP - RF.
Smallest difference of interest (margin): 0.01 AUC.
Primary procedure: CR2 with Bell-McCaffrey degrees of freedom, clusters = cells, on 5-seed means; 95 %.
Reference population: test cells with 10-60 rows. Reference estimate: the contrast on all of them, with its CR2
  interval. (The contrast on all test cells is also reported.)
Small tests: 1,000 draws of 19 cells without replacement from the reference population (about 500 rows, like the
  first panel of the paper). In each draw: is the contrast resolved by CR2 / by DeLong, with which sign; does the
  CR2 interval cover the reference estimate.

Predictions:
P1  C1 (RF - LR) is resolved in the reference test, with a difference above the margin.
P2  In the small tests, CR2 resolves C1 in at least 80 % of draws, and a resolution with the wrong sign occurs in
    at most 2.5 % of draws for every contrast.
P3  The coverage of the reference estimate by the small-test CR2 interval is 0.93-0.97 for every contrast.
P4  For contrasts whose reference difference is within the margin, the small-test resolution rate of CR2 is at most
    10 %; DeLong resolves at least as often as CR2 for every contrast (it ignores the spatial dependence).
P5  The protocol MDE predicted from the reference test by N^-1/2 scaling is within a factor 0.7-1.4 of the
    standard deviation-based MDE (2.80 x SD of the small-test estimates) for each contrast.
