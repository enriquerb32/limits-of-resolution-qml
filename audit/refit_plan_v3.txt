Refit v3 of the first panel - plan fixed before the new runs (2026-11-23)
==========================================================================
Status of this plan: the results of the archived pipeline (v2) are known to the author, so this is a plan fixed before
the NEW runs, not a blind confirmation of the original panel. Its purpose is to remove the two main objections of the
review of 2026-11-23: a primary pipeline with known lookahead (point 4) and an unequal training budget between the
circuit, its controls and the trees (point 5). Every deviation from this plan will be listed in the supplement.

1. Pipeline
  * Primary: causal imputation (V2_CAUSAL=1): every predictor of the window for target month t uses data up to the
    end of t-1 only. The archived pipeline (v2) becomes a sensitivity analysis; the archived-versus-causal comparison
    is kept, on the common rows and on the full causal sample.
  * Clean run: a new worktree with empty results/, logs_v2/ and checkpoints/exp31/; only input data are copied
    (cleaned daily CSV, second-panel daily files, SEAS5 member files). Tree hyperparameters are re-tuned on the causal
    pipeline (the archived v2_tuned_params.json is not read). After each phase, an audit checks that every output was
    written after the phase started and that no archived prediction, checkpoint or resume file was read.
  * Splits, seeds 0-14, labels (pooled training 60th percentile) and test rows as in v2.

2. Training budget (one search, the same for every neural model)
  Models searched: LSTM, LSTM+QNN, LSTM+QTL, Linear-4D, Fourier-1 to -4 (separable), Full Fourier-1, -2 and -4
  (tensor bases of 81, 625 and 6,561 functions of the four angles). Frozen circuits are not searched (they take the
  LSTM+QNN setting).
  * Optuna TPE, 30 trials per model, sampler seed 0; one training seed per trial (seed 100, outside the final 0-14).
  * Selection metric: validation AUC of the trial (the metric used to tune the trees).
  * Search space, identical for every model: learning rate log-uniform [1e-3, 3e-2]; weight decay log-uniform
    [1e-5, 1e-2]; maximum epochs {25, 50, 100}; early-stopping patience {6, 15}; batch size {16, 32}. Dropout keeps the
    value of the shared LSTM backbone (not searched, since it is common to the circuit and its controls).
  * Final fits: the selected setting, 15 seeds, each the best of 3 random starts by validation loss (restarts for
    every model, not only the circuit).
  * The range of the search space was informed by a pilot on planted signals (exp64_circuit_budget_pilot.py, circuit
    head only, results archived); the pilot did not use the panel labels.
  * Trees: Optuna TPE, 50 trials, validation AUC, on the causal pipeline, as in v2.

3. Primary metrics and decision rules
  * Two co-primary metrics: the pooled AUC (as before) and the within-station AUC (positive-negative pairs of the same
    station only; per-station AUCs averaged with weights n_pos x n_neg). The within-station AUC removes comparisons
    between stations, not the seasonality within each station; the station x month threshold stays a separate
    sensitivity analysis.
  * Inference for both: CR2 with Bell-McCaffrey degrees of freedom over calendar years, on 15-seed means; the
    within-station AUC difference uses its own influence values, summed by year.
  * A difference between two models is called resolved only if BOTH co-primary CR2 intervals exclude zero with the
    same sign (an intersection-union rule, valid at level 0.05 without a further correction). Holm is applied within
    each of the two primary families, separately for each metric; resolved after Holm requires both metrics to pass.
    If only one metric resolves, the difference is reported as metric-dependent, not resolved.
  * Equivalence: TOST with CR2 and margin 0.01 AUC, on both metrics; equivalence is claimed only if both pass.
  * Floor of protocol item 1 and two- or three-year clusters: sensitivity analyses, as in v2.

4. Circuit versus controls, and recovery of the teacher
  * Full Fourier-4 is a containment control: with the same interface and post-processing, its basis contains the
    outputs of the LSTM+QNN circuit, but its free linear map can represent more functions. It is not a control of equal
    expressivity or capacity.
  * Main teacher test: the repeated planted-signal control of exp50 (teacher score through the same LayerNorm as the
    students, same angles; candidates 0-3, oracle test AUC 0.70 and 0.85, two nulls). The single teacher of exp38 uses
    a score WITHOUT LayerNorm and is not used as the main test. Heads: circuit, Linear-4D, Fourier-2, Full Fourier-1
    and Full Fourier-4, each with the setting selected in section 2, 5 seeds.
  * A head 'recovers' the teacher in a configuration if its 5-seed mean test AUC is at least the oracle AUC - 0.03.
  * Circuit versus Full Fourier-4: paired CR2 difference of 5-seed means per configuration.
  * Interpretation rules, fixed now:
    - both recover: their performance is compared under the fixed budget, and nothing more;
    - one does not recover: the difference is reported as a property of the training procedure, not as a limit of
      expressivity;
    - contrast not resolved: inconclusive, unless TOST with margin 0.01 AUC establishes equivalence.
  * On the panel, a quantum contribution is claimed only if the circuit is resolved above every interface control
    (both co-primary metrics, Holm over the interface contrasts). Exact emulation is a property of the spectrum and is
    not evidence for or against a contribution.

5. What does not change
  The second panel, SEAS5 and the external benchmarks are not refitted under this plan (the external benchmark v2 has
  its own record, audit/prediction_external_v2.txt). If the results are null or inconclusive, no further round of
  experiments is opened.
